How to Play GLYPHS

1

Place Tiles on the Board

Drag or click a tile from your hand, then place it on any empty board cell. You can build words in any horizontal (left-to-right) or vertical (top-to-bottom) line.

2

Watch Words Glow

Whenever you form a valid English word (3+ letters), the word will pulse with a vibrant orange outline, displaying its calculated score.

3

Harvest or Extend

Tap the glowing word or the COLLECT banner to harvest it. Harvesting clears the tiles, frees up board space, and triggers a Combo multiplier! Or extend the word into a longer word for a massive length bonus!

4

Master Modifiers

Utilize compound digraphs (TH, CH, QU), 2× multiplier cells, Wildcard Prisms, and Purge Bombs to rack up legendary high scores before your deck runs out!
